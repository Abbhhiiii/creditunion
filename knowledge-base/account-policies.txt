CREDIT UNION ACCOUNT POLICIES
=====================================

1. SAVINGS ACCOUNT POLICIES
   - Minimum balance: $100
   - Monthly fee: None for balances above $500
   - Low balance fee: $2.50 if balance falls below $100
   - Interest rate: 0.75% APY
   - Transaction limits: Unlimited withdrawals
   - Account opening time: 24 hours

2. CHECKING ACCOUNT POLICIES
   - Minimum balance: $50
   - Monthly fee: None
   - Overdraft fee: $35 per transaction
   - Overdraft protection available: Yes
   - Check writing: Unlimited
   - Debit card included: Yes

3. SAVINGS GOALS ACCOUNT
   - Purpose: Save for specific goals
   - Interest rate: 1.25% APY
   - Minimum deposit: $50
   - Maximum sub-accounts: 5
   - Withdrawal restrictions: Can withdraw anytime
   - Perfect for: Vacations, down payments, emergencies

4. ACCOUNT CLOSURE POLICY
   - Notice period: No notice required
   - Processing time: 5-7 business days
   - Final balance transfer: Free to any account
   - Outstanding transactions: Must be cleared first
   - Reason for closure: Optional

5. ACCOUNT HOLDER REQUIREMENTS
   - Minimum age: 18 years
   - Valid ID required: Passport, Driver's License, or Government ID
   - Address proof: Utility bill or rent agreement
   - Non-members: Can become members within 24 hours

6. BENEFICIARY NOMINATION
   - Allowed for: All account types
   - Maximum nominees: 4 per account
   - Nominee change: Anytime without penalty
   - Nominee documentation: Valid ID and address proof

7. JOINT ACCOUNT RULES
   - Either party can operate independently
   - Both signatures not required for transactions
   - Death of one party: Account operates normally
   - Nomination: Separate nomination possible
   - Interest distribution: Equal or as per agreement
