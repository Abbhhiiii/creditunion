CREDIT UNION LOAN PRODUCTS
===========================

1. PERSONAL LOANS
   - Loan amount: $500 to $50,000
   - Interest rate: 7.5% to 15% APR (based on credit score)
   - Tenure: 6 months to 7 years
   - Processing fee: 1% of loan amount
   - Prepayment: No penalty
   - Documents required: Income proof, ID, address proof
   - Approval time: 24-48 hours
   - Co-applicant: Optional

2. HOME LOANS
   - Loan amount: $50,000 to $1,000,000
   - Interest rate: 4.5% to 6.5% APR
   - Tenure: 10 to 30 years
   - Down payment: Minimum 20%
   - Processing fee: 1.5% of loan amount
   - Insurance: Mandatory
   - Documents: 2 years income tax returns, employment letter
   - Approval time: 15-30 days
   - Home valuation: Required

3. AUTO LOANS
   - Vehicle value: $5,000 to $250,000
   - Interest rate: 5.5% to 12.5% APR
   - Tenure: 3 to 7 years
   - Down payment: Minimum 15%
   - Insurance: Mandatory
   - Gap insurance: Available
   - Approval time: 3-5 days
   - Documents: ID, income proof, vehicle details

4. EDUCATION LOANS
   - Course period: Up to 7 years in repayment
   - Loan amount: $5,000 to $250,000
   - Interest rate: 4.5% to 8.5% APR
   - Moratorium: Up to 6 months after completion
   - Co-applicant: Usually required (parent/guardian)
   - Eligible courses: Accredited institutions
   - Processing fee: 0.5% of loan amount
   - Approval time: 7-10 days

5. BUSINESS LOANS
   - Business type: Sole proprietor to Partnership
   - Loan amount: $10,000 to $500,000
   - Interest rate: 8% to 15% APR
   - Tenure: 1 to 10 years
   - Documents: Business registration, 2 years ITR, balance sheet
   - Business plan: Required
   - Collateral: May be required
   - Approval time: 15-25 days

6. LOAN ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
   - Age: 21 to 65 years
   - Income: Minimum $24,000/year
   - Employment: Minimum 2 years in current job
   - CIBIL score: Minimum 700
   - Debt-to-income ratio: Maximum 50%
   - Existing loan: Maximum 3 active loans

7. LOAN REPAYMENT OPTIONS
   - Monthly installments: Standard
   - Bi-weekly: Available for salaried
   - Flexible repayment: Available
   - Bullet repayment: For business loans
   - Partial prepayment: No penalty
   - Full prepayment: No penalty after 6 months
   - EMI calculator: Available on portal

8. DEFAULT AND PENALTY
   - Late payment: $25 penalty
   - Cheque bouncing: $50 per instance
   - EMI default (3+ months): CIBIL reporting
   - Legal action: After 6 months default
   - Recovery charges: As per RBI guidelines
