CREDIT UNION CARD SERVICES
===========================

1. DEBIT CARD FEATURES
   - Annual fee: $0
   - Replacement fee: $0 for first 2 replacements
   - Daily withdrawal limit: $500
   - Online transaction limit: $2,000/day
   - International transactions: Allowed
   - Currency conversion: 2.5% mark-up
   - Chip + PIN security: Yes
   - Contactless payment: Yes

2. CREDIT CARD OPTIONS
   - Credit limit: $1,000 to $100,000
   - Interest rate: 12% to 28% APR
   - Annual fee: $0 - $150 (based on tier)
   - Grace period: 45 days
   - Minimum payment: 5% of outstanding balance
   - Rewards: 1-2 points per $1 spent
   - Rewards redemption: Cash back, travel, shopping

3. CARD BLOCKING/UNBLOCKING
   - Blocked reason: Transaction limit exceeded, lost, stolen, fraud
   - Block time: Immediate upon request
   - Unblock time: 24 hours online request, instant call center
   - Temporary block: Available for 24 hours
   - Documents needed: ID verification only
   - Online process: Self-service portal
   - Phone process: 24/7 helpline

4. CARD REPLACEMENT
   - Lost card: Immediate block, replacement in 7 days
   - Damaged card: Replacement in 5 days
   - Expired card: Automatic issue 30 days before expiry
   - Address change: Reissued to new address
   - Name change: Reissued with new name
   - Fee: $0 for first replacement

5. EMV AND SECURITY FEATURES
   - CVV protection: Required for online payments
   - 3D Secure: Enabled for international transactions
   - OTP verification: For online transactions above $200
   - Tokenization: Supported for repeat merchants
   - Fraud protection: $0 liability for unauthorized use
   - Encryption: 256-bit SSL

6. CARD ACTIVATION
   - New card activation: Online or phone required
   - Activation time: Immediate
   - Online activation: Portal login required
   - Phone activation: 24/7 helpline
   - International use: Enabled after activation
   - PIN setting: Online or ATM
   - Deactivation: Available anytime

7. PIN MANAGEMENT
   - Change PIN: Online, ATM, or phone
   - Forgot PIN: Online reset or visit branch
   - PIN attempts: 3 attempts, then locked for 24 hours
   - PIN length: 4 digits
   - PIN reset fee: $0
   - Speed: Instant for online/phone

8. CARD STATEMENT AND BILLING
   - Statement delivery: Monthly
   - Online statement: Real-time access
   - Paper statement fee: $0 for first 12 months
   - Payment due date: 21 days from statement
   - Autopay available: Yes
   - Paperless option: Yes
   - Statement period: Calendar month
