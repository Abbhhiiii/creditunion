ACCOUNT MANAGEMENT PROCEDURES
=============================

1. ACCOUNT OPENING PROCEDURE
   - Step 1: Visit online portal or branch
   - Step 2: Fill application form
   - Step 3: Submit ID and address proof
   - Step 4: Video KYC verification (online) or in-person
   - Step 5: Agree to terms and conditions
   - Step 6: Account activated within 24 hours
   - Documents: Valid ID, address proof, PAN (if available)
   - Processing fee: $0

2. KNOW YOUR CUSTOMER (KYC) REQUIREMENTS
   - Primary document: Passport, Aadhar, Driver's License, Voter ID
   - Address proof: Utility bill, rent agreement, bank statement
   - PAN: Optional for first $25,000/year transactions
   - Income proof: Salary slip, ITR, business registration
   - Verification: Video call or in-person
   - Update requirements: When details change

3. ADDRESS UPDATE PROCEDURE
   - Online process: Portal self-service (24 hours)
   - Phone process: Customer service (1-2 days)
   - Branch process: In-person with document (same day)
   - Documents needed: Utility bill or government ID
   - Fee: $0
   - Effective from: Within 24 hours

4. NAME CHANGE PROCEDURE
   - Reason: Marriage, deed poll, court order
   - Documents: Marriage certificate, deed poll, or court order
   - Processing time: 5-7 business days
   - Fee: $0
   - New cards issued: Yes, with updated name
   - Notification: Updated in all accounts

5. ACCOUNT TRANSFER (TO ANOTHER PERSON)
   - Only joint account holders can claim
   - Death of primary holder: Automatically to co-holder
   - Otherwise: Not transferable
   - Nomination: Can be changed anytime
   - Successor: Can be nominated for automatic transfer

6. ACCOUNT REACTIVATION
   - Dormant account: No transactions for 2 years
   - Reactivation process: Contact customer service
   - Documents: ID verification
   - Fee: $0 for reactivation
   - Time: 24 hours
   - Balance: Preserved from dormancy

7. ACCOUNT UPGRADE/DOWNGRADE
   - Upgrade: Anytime based on eligibility
   - Downgrade: Anytime, subject to minimum requirements
   - Fee: $0
   - Time: Immediate
   - Benefits: Automatically updated
   - Notification: Via email and SMS

8. TRANSACTION LIMITS
   - Daily ATM withdrawal: $500
   - Daily online transfer: $10,000
   - Daily debit card usage: $2,000
   - Monthly transfer: Unlimited
   - Standing order: Unlimited
   - Limits by: Account type and balance

9. BULK PAYMENT PROCEDURES
   - File format: CSV, Excel, or bank-specific
   - File submission: Online portal or branch
   - Validation: Automatic checks for errors
   - Processing time: Same-day or next-day
   - Batch limit: Maximum 1,000 transactions
   - Fee: $0.25 per transaction (minimum $5)

10. STATEMENT AND RECORD PROCEDURES
    - Digital statement: Free, available anytime
    - Paper statement: Free for first 12 months
    - Statement format: PDF downloadable online
    - Statement archival: Available for 7 years
    - Certificate of balance: $0 per copy
    - Credit certificate: $0 per copy
