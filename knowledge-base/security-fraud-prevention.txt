SECURITY AND FRAUD PREVENTION
==============================

1. PHISHING AND SCAM PREVENTION
   - Never ask: Password, PIN, CVV in email/phone
   - Verify: Always call official number back
   - URL check: Look for HTTPS and correct domain
   - Attachment: Don't download from unknown sources
   - Links: Hover to check actual URL before clicking
   - Grammar: Scams often have spelling errors
   - Urgency: Be suspicious of "act now" pressure

2. CARD SECURITY BEST PRACTICES
   - Keep card safe: Don't share card details
   - Store receipt: Don't discard with card info visible
   - Monitor transactions: Check regularly for unauthorized use
   - Deactivate: When replacing card
   - Chip use: Prefer chip over swipe when possible
   - Contactless limit: $50 without PIN verification
   - Online shopping: Use strong password-protected sites

3. ONLINE BANKING SECURITY
   - Strong password: Minimum 8 chars, mixed case, numbers, special
   - 2-FA enabled: Use authenticator app preferred over SMS
   - Browser security: Update browser and OS regularly
   - Public WiFi: Avoid for banking transactions
   - Log out: Always after session
   - Device security: Use antivirus and firewall
   - Session timeout: 15 minutes

4. FRAUD REPORTING
   - Immediate action: Call 1-800-UNION-CU immediately
   - Card fraud: Report within 24 hours
   - Account fraud: Report as soon as discovered
   - Unauthorized transfer: Report before deadline
   - Document: Keep proof of reporting
   - Fraud alert: Free credit freeze available
   - Monitor account: Check regularly

5. FRAUD PROTECTION GUARANTEE
   - Unauthorized cards: $0 liability if reported
   - Fraudulent transfer: Full refund (conditions apply)
   - Phishing: Full account protection if acted quickly
   - Chargeback fraud: Fighting your corner
   - Identity theft: Credit monitoring offered
   - Liability: Limited by federal law

6. IDENTITY THEFT PROTECTION
   - Credit freeze: Available at all 3 bureaus
   - Fraud alert: 1 year (7 years for victim)
   - Monitoring: Free for credit union members
   - Lost documents: Report to authorities
   - Social security: Protect your SSN
   - Data breach: Notification within 30 days
   - Recovery: Step-by-step assistance provided

7. SAFE TRANSACTION PRACTICES
   - Cover PIN: When entering at ATM or POS
   - Different passwords: For different sites
   - Biometric: Enable fingerprint/face recognition
   - Verification: Call number from statement for confirmation
   - Recent activity: Review regularly
   - Devices: Don't use shared computers for banking
   - Backups: Save important confirmations

8. SUSPICIOUS ACTIVITY CHECKLIST
   - Account locked after few wrong attempts
   - Unexpected transactions appearing
   - Cards not received in 7 days
   - Change of address confirmation you didn't request
   - New cards arriving you didn't order
   - Account statements missing
   - Bills not arriving
   - Calls about accounts you don't have

Report any of these immediately!
