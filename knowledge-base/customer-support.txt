CUSTOMER SUPPORT AND CONTACT
============================

1. CONTACT CHANNELS
   - Phone: 1-800-UNION-CU (1-800-864-6628)
   - Email: support@creditunion.com
   - WhatsApp: +1-555-0100
   - Live chat: Available 9 AM - 9 PM EST
   - Social media: Twitter, Facebook, Instagram
   - Branch: 500+ locations nationwide
   - Mobile app: In-app chat support

2. PHONE SUPPORT
   - Availability: 24/7 for card/fraud, 8 AM - 6 PM others
   - Wait time: Average 5 minutes
   - Language: English, Spanish, Hindi, Kannada
   - Account access: Verify with account number or SSN
   - Recording: Calls are recorded for quality
   - Escalation: Available for complex issues

3. EMAIL SUPPORT
   - Response time: Within 48 hours
   - General: support@creditunion.com
   - Loans: loans@creditunion.com
   - Disputes: disputes@creditunion.com
   - Complaints: complaints@creditunion.com
   - Encryption: PGP available for sensitive info
   - Attachment: Max 10 MB

4. BRANCH SERVICES
   - Service hours: Monday-Friday 9-5, Saturday 9-1
   - Holiday: Closed on federal holidays
   - Available services: All banking + document signing
   - Appointment: Online or phone
   - Walk-ins: Welcome
   - Video banking: For remote branches
   - Video call: Available in remote areas

5. FAQ AND KNOWLEDGE BASE
   - Online access: creditunion.com/help
   - Categories: Accounts, cards, loans, transfers
   - Search: Keyword-based search
   - Video tutorials: For major services
   - Community forum: User discussions
   - Updates: Regular content update
   - Feedback: Submit question if not found

6. COMPLAINT FILING PROCESS
   - Online: Portal complaint form
   - Phone: Customer service (formal letter option)
   - Email: complaints@creditunion.com
   - Branch: In-person filing
   - Resolution time: 30 days
   - Acknowledgment: Within 24 hours
   - Updates: Weekly notification

7. RBI OMBUDSMAN COMPLAINT
   - When: After internal resolution not satisfactory
   - Website: rbiombudsman.rbi.org.in
   - Fee: Completely free
   - Timeline: Within 3 months of escalation
   - Decision: Binding on bank, non-binding on customer
   - Appeal: Customer can appeal to higher authority
   - Categories: Service failures, errors, unauthorized transaction

8. FEEDBACK AND SUGGESTIONS
   - Method: Online portal, phone, email
   - Incentive: Monthly drawing for feedback entries
   - Prize: Up to $100 gift card
   - Anonymity: Option available
   - Response: Team reviews all feedback
   - Implementation: Popular suggestions implemented quarterly
