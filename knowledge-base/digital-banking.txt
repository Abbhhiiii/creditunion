DIGITAL BANKING SERVICES
==========================

1. MOBILE BANKING APP
   - Features: View balance, transfer money, pay bills
   - Download: iOS and Android
   - Security: Biometric login, encryption
   - Available 24/7: Yes
   - Transaction limit: Up to $10,000 per day
   - Fund transfer: To own accounts or other banks
   - Bill payments: Utilities, credit cards, insurance

2. INTERNET BANKING (ONLINE PORTAL)
   - Access: login.creditunion.com
   - Browser: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge
   - Two-factor authentication: Required
   - Features: Account view, transfers, statements
   - Mobile responsive: Yes
   - Session timeout: 15 minutes idle
   - Password: Change every 90 days

3. LOGIN AND SECURITY
   - Username: Email or account number
   - Password: Minimum 8 characters, alphanumeric + special
   - Two-FA: SMS OTP or authenticator app
   - Biometric: Fingerprint or face recognition
   - Device registration: New device needs verification
   - Lost password: Reset via email (24 hours)
   - Session logout: Auto-logout after 15 minutes

4. FUND TRANSFER OPTIONS
   - RTGS (Real-time): Above $10,000, instant
   - NEFT (Delayed): Up to $10,000, 1-2 hours
   - IMPS (Immediate): Up to $100,000, instant (24/7)
   - UPI: Via Unified Payment Interface
   - Domestic transfer: Free to own accounts
   - Outstation transfer: Free for NEFT/RTGS
   - International transfer: $25 fee + currency conversion

5. UPI PAYMENT SYSTEM
   - Available on: Mobile app and third-party apps
   - Features: Send/receive money, QR code payments
   - Transaction limit: $100,000 per transaction
   - Daily limit: Unlimited transactions
   - Security: PIN-based
   - Registration: Mobile number + bank account
   - Virtual address: username@bankname format

6. BILL PAYMENT AND UTILITIES
   - Available billers: Over 15,000 companies
   - Categories: Electricity, water, gas, telecom, insurance
   - Auto-pay: Set recurring payments
   - Receipt: Digital receipt available
   - Processing: Same day or next day
   - Fee: Free for most services
   - Payment proof: SMS and email confirmation

7. CHEQUE MANAGEMENT
   - Checkbook request: Online or branch
   - Processing time: 5-7 days
   - Cheque printing: Free, standard design
   - Stop payment: Online or phone, $30 fee
   - Cheque status: Online tracking
   - Cheque deposit: Mobile app image deposit
   - Clearing time: 2-3 days for local, 5-7 days outstation

8. E-STATEMENTS AND ARCHIVAL
   - Available from: Online portal
   - Format: PDF downloadable
   - Period: Last 7 years
   - Frequency: Monthly
   - Email: Auto-delivery option
   - Print: Download and print
   - Authentication: Digital signature available

9. ACCOUNT ALERTS
   - Available alerts: Balance, transaction, statement
   - Delivery: SMS and email
   - Customization: Turn on/off in portal
   - Threshold alerts: Set custom amount limits
   - Transaction alerts: For all transactions
   - Statement alert: Monthly reminder
   - Security alert: Login from new device
