DISPUTE RESOLUTION PROCESS
============================

1. TRANSACTION DISPUTE PROCESS
   - Filing deadline: 90 days from transaction
   - Documentation needed: Transaction receipt, identity proof
   - Investigation period: 10-15 working days
   - Merchant contact: Yes, for online transactions
   - Refund timeline: 3-5 business days after resolution
   - Appeal option: Yes, within 30 days
   - Fee for dispute: $0 (free)

2. DISPUTE CATEGORIES
   - Unauthorized transaction
   - Duplicate charge
   - Service not provided
   - Partial refund claim
   - Billing error
   - Card not used transactions
   - Phishing/fraud

3. DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS
   - Original transaction receipt
   - Communication with merchant
   - Proof of payment (if applicable)
   - Police FIR (for fraud cases)
   - Bank statement showing transaction
   - Any evidence of non-delivery

4. FRAUD CLAIM PROCESS
   - Immediate reporting: Within 24 hours of discovery
   - Card blocking: Automatic for fraud claims
   - Police complaint: Required for amounts > $500
   - Investigation: 30-45 days
   - Provisional credit: Available within 10 days
   - Final resolution: Within 90 days

5. CHARGEBACK PROCESS
   - Applicable for: Credit card transactions
   - Timeline: Within 120 days of transaction
   - Documentation: Proof of non-delivery
   - Merchant response: 30 days allowed
   - Final decision: Made by card network
   - Appeal available: Yes

6. COMPLAINT ESCALATION
   - Level 1: Customer service (5 days)
   - Level 2: Department manager (10 days)
   - Level 3: Executive level (15 days)
   - RBI Ombudsman: After Level 3 resolution
   - RBI Portal: complaints.rbi.org.in
   - No fee: For any complaint

7. SETTLEMENT OPTIONS
   - Full refund
   - Partial refund
   - Credit to account
   - Store credit (if merchant authorized)
   - Goodwill settlement
   - Replacement of service

8. RESOLUTION STATUS TRACKING
   - Online portal: Real-time updates
   - Email notifications: At each stage
   - SMS alerts: For major updates
   - Customer helpline: For queries
   - Case reference number: Provided immediately
