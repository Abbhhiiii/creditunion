CREDIT SCORE AND CIBIL INFORMATION
===================================

1. WHAT IS CREDIT SCORE
   - Definition: Numerical rating of creditworthiness
   - Range: 300-900
   - Provider: CIBIL, Equifax, Experian, CRIF High Mark
   - Update frequency: Monthly
   - Free check: Available on RBI portal
   - Purpose: Determine loan eligibility

2. SCORE RANGES AND MEANING
   - 750-900: Excellent (Easy approval)
   - 700-749: Good (Likely approval)
   - 650-699: Fair (Conditional approval)
   - 600-649: Poor (Difficult approval)
   - Below 600: Very Poor (Likely rejection)
   - No history: Neutral (First-time applicants)

3. FACTORS AFFECTING CREDIT SCORE
   - Payment history: 35% (Most important)
   - Credit utilization: 30% (Keep below 30%)
   - Length of history: 15% (Longer is better)
   - Credit mix: 10% (Variety of credit types)
   - New credit inquiries: 10% (Fewer is better)

4. IMPACT OF LOAN AND CREDIT
   - New loan: Decreases score by 10-50 points temporarily
   - Missed payment: Decreases by 100-150 points
   - Default (3+ months): Major decrease, reporting to authorities
   - Credit card payment: Increases score when on time
   - Zero balance: Does not improve score much
   - Account closure: May reduce score slightly

5. IMPROVING CREDIT SCORE
   - Pay bills on time: Most important action
   - Reduce credit card balance: Keep below 30%
   - Increase credit limit: Request increase without hard inquiry
   - Mix of credit: Maintain different types of credit
   - Don't apply for multiple cards: Limit hard inquiries
   - Dispute errors: Incorrect entries can be challenged
   - Regular monitoring: Check annually

6. HOW CREDIT SCORE AFFECTS LOANS
   - Score 750+: Lowest interest rate, instant approval
   - Score 700-749: Standard interest rate, quick approval
   - Score 650-699: Slightly higher rate, additional documents
   - Score 600-649: Higher rate, requires collateral
   - Below 600: Very high rate or rejection
   - Special cases: Can get loans with co-applicant

7. FREE CREDIT SCORE CHECK
   - CIBIL website: cibil.com (1 free annual report)
   - RBI portal: mfin.rbi.org.in (Free annual check)
   - Bank portal: Often provides free score check
   - Credit card company: Many provide free monthly monitoring
   - Response file: Available within 30 days of request

8. DISPUTE RESOLUTION FOR CREDIT SCORE
   - Error identification: Check for incorrect entries
   - Dispute filing: File with credit bureau directly
   - Documentation: Provide proof of error
   - Investigation: Bureau reviews within 30 days
   - Resolution: Correction reflected within 15 days
   - Appeal: If not resolved, escalate to RBI

9. CREDIT SCORE MAINTENANCE AFTER LOAN
   - During loan: Make timely EMI payments
   - Impact: On-time payments improve score
   - Full payment: Improve score significantly
   - Prepayment: Good for score, reduces interest
   - After closure: Account shows on report for 7 years
   - Reference: Useful for future loan applications
