CREDIT UNION INTEREST RATES & CHARGES
======================================

1. SAVINGS ACCOUNT INTEREST RATES
   - Standard rate: 0.75% APY
   - High balance (>$10K): 1.0% APY
   - Very high balance (>$100K): 1.25% APY
   - Senior citizens: Additional 0.25%
   - Interest credited: Monthly (last business day)
   - Minimum balance for interest: $100

2. CHECKING ACCOUNT RATES
   - Interest rate: 0.10% APY (non-interest bearing checking available)
   - Interest earning minimum: $1,000 balance
   - Interest credited: Quarterly
   - Promotional rate: 0.5% APY for first 6 months

3. FIXED DEPOSITS (CERTIFICATE OF DEPOSIT)
   - 3-month rate: 4.5% APY
   - 6-month rate: 4.75% APY
   - 1-year rate: 5.0% APY
   - 2-year rate: 5.25% APY
   - 3-year rate: 5.5% APY
   - 5-year rate: 5.75% APY
   - Minimum deposit: $500

4. CREDIT CARD INTEREST RATES
   - Purchase APR: 12% - 28%
   - Cash advance APR: 20% - 35%
   - Balance transfer APR: 5.99% - 22.99% (intro rates available)
   - Late payment APR: Up to 38% (penalty rate)
   - Grace period: 45 days (no interest if paid in full)

5. LOAN INTEREST RATES
   - Personal loans: 7.5% - 15% APR
   - Home loans: 4.5% - 6.5% APR
   - Auto loans: 5.5% - 12.5% APR
   - Education loans: 4.5% - 8.5% APR
   - Business loans: 8% - 15% APR
   - Rates based on: Credit score, loan amount, tenure

6. OVERDRAFT CHARGES
   - Overdraft fee: $35 per transaction
   - Monthly overdraft fee: $12 (max 3 per month)
   - Overdraft interest rate: 18% APR
   - Overdraft protection: $100 - $5,000 (optional)
   - Overdraft limit: Based on account balance

7. LATE PAYMENT AND OTHER FEES
   - Late payment fee: $25
   - Wire transfer: $15
   - Stop payment: $30
   - Check printing: $20 per 50 checks
   - ATM withdrawal (non-network): $2
   - Account research: $25 per hour
   - Returned check fee: $25
   - Monthly maintenance: None (with $500+ balance)

8. PROMOTIONAL RATES
   - New account bonus: 1% extra for 3 months
   - Referral rate: 0.5% additional for referred accounts
   - Student rate: Additional 0.25% for students
   - Military rate: Additional 0.5% for active military
   - Senior rate: Additional 0.25% for 65+
   - Holiday bonus: Seasonal promotional rates

9. RATE CHANGES
   - Notice period: 30 days before any decrease
   - Locked rates: Available for fixed term products
   - Variable rates: Can change with prime rate
   - Market rate: Linked to Federal Reserve rates
   - Rate review: Quarterly for variable accounts
